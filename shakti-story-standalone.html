01 — The starting point

Every check is green.

The product manager is ready to ship an agentic app built for a business team.

She has already tested its agentic workflows, functionality, and the sources behind its AI outputs. Everything is working well.

02 — The unanswered question

“How may it feel to use?”

She wants to feel confident about the end user experience before customers get their hands on it.

Will people understand the AI output? Will they trust a recommendation? What happens when they need more context, or have very little patience?

03 — Meet Shakti

A way to simulate human variation

She turns to Shakti: Synthetic Human Agent for Knowledge, Testing and Insight.

Shakti orchestrates synthetic users to explore a variety of human behaviours at scale.

04 — Set the context

Three inputs. A shared starting point.

Shakti takes the three inputs, a user role, a business outcome, and the environment in which people will use the app.

These inputs set the context for every synthetic user in the study.

05 — Write in the role

Start with the user role.

She provides the user role, an operations analyst, to Shakti.

06 — Write in the shared purpose

Business outcome

For this, she provides what that person would care about: identify and prioritize the items that matter, with the information they need to decide where to focus.

07 — Set the conditions

Environmental variables

She gives Shakti the environmental variables to explore: how people interact, their accessibility needs, and the device or viewport they use.

08 — Create the people

Shakti at work

With those inputs, Shakti creates a cohort of synthetic users with varied personality profiles.

They share a job role and business goal. But each brings a different way of communicating, consuming information, building trust, and responding to friction.

09 — A glimpse of behaviour

Listen to how they begin.

A few users come into focus. One asks for a short answer; another explains the full situation.

One wants to inspect the evidence. Another is ready to act on the recommendation.

10 — Another glimpse

Now, meet a different group.

As the first group fades, other users come into view.

Some read the details; others skim. Some work through an incomplete answer; others quickly lose patience.

11 — Add human variation

Eight dimensions. Many combinations.

A shared role does not mean a shared approach. Communication, decisions, risk, trust, and control all vary from person to person.

So do AI literacy, information consumption, and frustration tolerance. Shakti combines these eight dimensions to create varied synthetic users.

12 — Let each person try

Synthetic testing in action.

Each synthetic user combines all eight dimensions and takes a turn with the agentic app.

Based on the requirement Shakti creates n synthetic users to test the agentic app by attempting to complete the business outcome. Each interactions remains in a sandbox so that there is no context sharing to avoid any biases.

13 — Capture the evidence

Every session leaves a record.

All the interaction, friction, goal completion data, interaction with AI and transcripts get recorded.

Each record stays connected to the synthetic user and the environment they used.

14 — Evaluate the experience

Evaluation of the session records

Shakti then uses various evaluation skills to go through the collected data and identify any issues related to the content, accessibility and experience.

15 — Bring it back to the PM

A report she can act on.

The product manager receives a report with prioritized issues, supporting evidence, affected user profiles, and conversation transcripts.

She can see what to improve, what to test again, and how to make the experience ready for customers.
